Securing a PDF document with a password protects your sensitive contracts, financial statements, or personal records from unauthorized access. The Protect PDF tool on YourOwnPDF.com applies AES-128 encryption entirely inside your browser, with no server involvement whatsoever. This means that Your files never leave your device, ensuring absolute privacy for sensitive financial sheets, corporate reports, or personal documents. There are no account registration requirements, no hidden fees, and zero server-side storage risks—giving you complete, uncompromised control of your secure virtual workspace.

1

1. Upload the PDF to Protect

Select a PDF from your computer or drag it into the upload zone. The tool accepts PDF files up to the 100MB total limit across all files. Your files never leave your device. They are processed directly on your device, meaning they are never sent to any server.

2

2. Set Password & Permission Rules

Once the file is loaded, the Settings panel appears automatically. Enter an Open Password — this is what recipients must type to open the PDF. Optionally set a separate Permissions Password that controls whether the owner can change restrictions. Use the checkboxes to allow or restrict printing, copying of text and images, and document editing or annotations.

3

3. Encrypt PDF in Your Browser

After setting your password and permissions, click the Protect PDF button. Tap the button to start. We will process everything locally on your device. It is fast and works even if you lose your internet connection.

4. Download Your Protected PDF

As soon as encryption finishes, your protected PDF is automatically downloaded to your device. Open the file in any PDF reader—Adobe Acrobat, Preview, or your browser—and it will prompt for the password you set. You can click Clear to reset the interface and protect another document. Your download is generated on-the-fly and remains 100% private.

The Protect PDF tool allows you to secure your sensitive PDF documents with strong password encryption directly inside your web browser. Operating entirely within your browser sandbox, this utility handles the complete encryption process locally, ensuring your passwords and document contents never leave your device.

Complete Control Over Security Permissions

Our encryption engine supports standard PDF owner and user password security features to protect your files:

  • User Password (Open Password): Setting a user password restricts file access, requiring anyone who opens the document to enter the designated password before viewing the contents.
  • Owner Password (Permissions Password): Setting an owner password locks editing, printing, and content copying capabilities. This ensures unauthorized users cannot alter or duplicate your data.
  • Lossless Security Integration: Because the document layers and embedded fonts are encrypted using native crypt protocols (RC4/AES equivalent structures) without rasterization, your document retains its original vectors and searchable texts.

100% Secure Client-Side Processing

Since the encryption engine runs directly inside your browser tab sandbox, your passwords and files are never transmitted over the network to a cloud server. Your security is maintained local and offline.
