3. Image-to-PDF Standardization: Preserving Visual Geometry

While PDFs serve as the universal standard for formal document exchange, visual media relies on a wide variety of raster and vector image formats. Each format was engineered for specific use cases. However, sharing raw image files individually often leads to formatting inconsistencies, accidental scaling errors, and printing issues. Converting images to a standardized PDF wrapper guarantees that your visual assets render uniformly across every device, operating system, and printer.
