The Split PDF tool provides you with the exact control you need to isolate or delete pages from your documents. Unlike tools that send your documents to a cloud server, this splitter works entirely in-browser. All files are compiled within your device's memory, ensuring 100% data security.

Two Splitting Modes to Choose From

To accommodate different document workflows, the Split PDF tool operates in two action modes:

  • Page Exclusion (Delete Pages): This mode is ideal when you want to remove unwanted page sheets from a document, such as scanning errors, cover pages, or disclaimers. You define the range of pages to discard, and the tool compiles the rest.
  • Page Extraction (Keep Selected Pages): Use this mode when you want to isolate specific pages, chapters, or reports from a larger PDF. The tool extracts only the page numbers you list and discards the rest.

Flexible Page Selection Syntax

The input range validator supports a variety of format strings to let you target exact pages:

  • 5 - Extracts or deletes page 5.
  • 1, 3, 5, 9 - Targets specific, individual page indices.
  • 5-12 - Targets all pages from page 5 to 12 inclusive.
  • 1-3, 7, 12-15 - Combines single pages and page ranges in a custom sequence.
  • 10-5 - Extracts pages in reverse order (e.g., from page 10 back to 5).

100% Private Client-Side Processing

Like all of our utilities, your files are processed completely offline. Your PDF content streams, text characters, and high-resolution images never cross the network. Your confidentiality and data security are fully guaranteed.
