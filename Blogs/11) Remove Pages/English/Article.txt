Common Mistakes to Avoid When Removing PagesDeleting pages seems simple enough, but a few easily overlooked mistakes can lead to extra work or corrupted output files. Watch out for these common missteps:⚠️ Warning: Confusing printed page labels with actual file index positions can lead to deleting the wrong content. Always check visual previews before downloading your edited file.1. Confusing Page Numbers with Visual Page LabelsThe Issue: A book's introduction might use Roman numerals (i, ii, iii), making printed page "1" actually the fourth physical page in the digital file container.The Fix: Rely on the structural index numbers displayed underneath each visual page thumbnail in your browser editor rather than the text printed inside the page layout.2. Using Lossy Image Converters for Page RemovalThe Issue: Printing a PDF to raster images, deleting unwanted image pages, and converting those images back to PDF strips interactive hyperlinks, selection layers, embedded fonts, and searchability.The Fix: Always use structural, DOM-level PDF manipulation tools that modify document object nodes directly without converting pages into flat images.3. Deleting Target Pages Out of SequenceThe Issue: Entering manual deletion lists without confirming page ranges can unintentionally remove crucial appendix sections or document signatory zones.The Fix: Use the batch input preview field to visually confirm your highlighted pages before clicking the final export button.14.
